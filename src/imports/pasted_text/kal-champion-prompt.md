# 01 — CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

KALCHAKRA is a fictional global racing championship celebrating the evolution of transportation.

The championship brings four eras of transportation onto one global racing stage:

### CLASS 01 — HERITAGE

🐂 **BULLOCK CART**

Traditional transportation preserved as a prestigious Heritage racing class.

### CLASS 02 — HUMAN POWER

🚲 **RACING CYCLE**

Human-powered speed, endurance and athletic performance.

### CLASS 03 — MOTOR

🏍️ **RACING MOTORCYCLE**

The transition from human power to mechanical power.

### CLASS 04 — FUTURE

🏎️ **ADVANCED RACING CAR**

The next generation of high-performance transportation.

The central idea is:

**PAST → PRESENT → FUTURE**

KALCHAKRA must feel like a serious international motorsport organization, not a student project and not a generic Formula 1 clone.

The **Heritage / Bullock Cart Grand Prix** is the most distinctive feature of the championship.

Treat it as prestigious, authentic, culturally rooted and emotionally powerful.

NEVER make the bullock cart:

* childish
* cartoonish
* comedic
* robotic
* modified into a science-fiction animal
* a gimmick

The animals must remain natural and authentic.

Technology should monitor the environment, timing, track conditions, safety and welfare rather than modifying the animals.
